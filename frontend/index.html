How a pack moves through Bidwright

  1. Receive

    Forward the client's email, drop a zip, or upload the files. Every document is stored unchanged, with its checksum, and rendered so it can be cited.

  2. Extract and cite

    The model lists every mention of typology, areas, services, stages, location, deadlines and terms, each tied to the exact lines on a page. Values it cannot place on a page are not accepted.

  3. Flag disagreements

    When the main RFP and the area schedule state different floor areas, you see both, side by side with their sources. The engine never picks one for you.

  4. Enrich from your history

    Units and regional stage names are normalised against your vocabulary. Location tier, billing currency, studio routing and bid rules come from your reference data, and comparable past proposals come from your archive.

  5. Review and confirm

    An estimator checks each value against its highlighted source, edits with a reason, and confirms. The confirmed version is locked, and every change is in an append-only log.

Know how accurate it is before you rely on it

Each workspace keeps a gold set: past RFP packs paired with the proposals your practice actually wrote. Change the model, the prompt or a rule, and Bidwright runs the whole set again and reports what moved.

Pricing-critical accuracy
Typology, areas, services, stage package and location, field by field against the gold answers.
Citation coverage
The share of extracted values that point to a highlighted line on a page.
Conflicts caught
Disagreements planted in the gold packs that were flagged, and any flagged that were not there.
Cost per pack
Model calls, tokens and spend for each run, by prompt version.

Your archive stays inside your workspace

Each practice gets its own workspace. Proposals, RFP packs, reference data and backtests belong to it, and the database itself refuses to return another workspace's rows. People join by invitation, with one of four roles.

RoleCan
OwnerEverything an admin can, plus appoint other owners and admins
AdminInvite people, edit reference data and bid rules, run backtests
EstimatorUpload RFP packs, correct values, resolve conflicts, confirm records
ViewerRead every record, source and backtest in the workspace
